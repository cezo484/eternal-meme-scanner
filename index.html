RugCanary

Scan a Solana token before you ape. Paste a mint, get the risk in plain English.

Try:

What it checks

  • Mint authority — can someone print more tokens?
  • Freeze authority — can someone freeze your balance?
  • Top holders — how much of the supply do the 10 biggest wallets own?
  • Liquidity & volume — is there a real market, or will a small sell crash the price?
  • Token age — how long has it been trading?

Risk score 0–100: 0–29 Low, 30–59 Medium, 60–100 High. Every point is explained next to the check that caused it. Data comes from the public Solana RPC and the DexScreener API, live, in your browser. No wallet connection, no sign-up, nothing is stored.

Not financial advice. A low score means no major red flags were found at scan time — not that a token is safe.
